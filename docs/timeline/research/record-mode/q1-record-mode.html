Record mode (armed)

Stays here until you press Space. Clicking marchers changes who's armed.

Select · Record→ Armed: field at the start→ Space: 4 clicks, move plays, drag→ Back to the start, still armed→ Space again = retake · Done = field at the end
  • Only what you drag changes (Logic's Touch). A take with no drag is just a playback.
  • Others play during the take, so layering one performer at a time comes free (record-arm).
  • Later, same mode: punch in/out (Expert's start flag to playhead), take history, then Leader and Rotation as what you arm.
  • Precedents: Logic/Ableton record-arm and count-in; After Effects Motion Sketch keeps the start frame on screen while you sketch; Blender's red auto-key button.
