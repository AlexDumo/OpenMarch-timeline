3 · Direction and the smallest first step

Record mode you arm, modeled on Edit move's bar and DAW record-arm. It owns the start view; there is no general start view.

First build (one step)

  1. Record (button or Alt+Shift+R) arms: the field shows the move's start, armed marchers ringed red, their current paths dashed to a hollow end dot.
  2. A bar over the field: who's armed, Space records a take, Done or Escape leaves.
  3. A take is today's take. Afterwards: back to the start, still armed, new path shown.
  4. Click marchers to re-arm. Drags before a take do nothing.

Left out on purpose

  • Next and Previous page while armed
  • Other marchers' paths while armed
  • Punch in/out, pre-roll, Expert window as the range
  • Take history, picking takes
  • Leader and rotation recording
  • Press-to-start, no count-in
  • Start-view toggle or hold-to-peek
  • Tablet details
Try: select 3 marchers on Page 2 · Record · look at the starting form · Space · drag · let it finish · Space for a retake · Done.
