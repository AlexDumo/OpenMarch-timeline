# Who goes where: options, picking by hand and Swap

When marchers move into a new shape, the writer decides which marcher takes which spot. Before
this change the floating editor offered Least travel, Keep order (only when every marcher was on
one placed Line) and Reverse order. This change adds more ways to choose, and a way to pick by hand.

The design was settled with the owner on 2026-11-25 through an interactive prototype (line to
arc, line to two arcs, two lines to an arc, 32 in a line, block to circle, and two moves where
the shapes overlap).

### Decisions made while building

These weren't spelled out in the prototype. Each is easy to change.

- **Keys.** W for Pick by hand ("who"), Shift+W for Swap two, Tab to switch between source and destination while picking. H was taken (Hide marchers). All three are in the command palette and Settings › Shortcuts (Tab hidden from the palette).
- **Two placed Lines** open on Least travel, as before. Keep order and Inside out are greyed there with "These marchers come from two lines. Use Zipper, or set one order on them first."
- **The new options have no picture** in the menu row: four marchers can't show them apart (Inside out of 1 2 3 4 reads 2 1 4 3, the same as the sample's Least travel). Every option's tip has a walking picture of seven instead (row 12).
- **Walking pictures are drawn in the app, not GIFs** (row 12): an SVG computed from the real assignment can't drift from the behavior, stays sharp at any zoom, follows the show's field colors, needs no text translated, and adds no binary files. Each option gets a scene that shows why you'd pick it (row 16), so pictures of different options don't start the same way or make the same form. The dots carry no drill numbers: on a piece of field, numbers read as the user's own marchers.
- **While picking, the shape's handles are hidden**: presses pick instead of editing the shape.
- **Picking has its own bar** (owner review, 2026-11-27): the floating editor (three rows) sat on a tall shape's spots while picking. Now its rows hide while picking or swapping and one row takes their place: the Who goes where button, Source / Destination, Tab, what waits, Done. You aren't reshaping then. It's kept in view (it went off the bottom under a shape running past the view) and off the spots, between two of them when it has to sit on the shape. Done brings the rows back. Pick by hand and Swap two are on the hidden row: W and Shift+W switch between them while picking, a mouse presses Done first.
- **Hand picks are dropped by a fresh draw or another kind**, and kept through another option, Recalculate and spacing changes.
- **Each pair is one undo step.** Undo while picking takes back a waiting pick first, then the last pair.
- **The readout** shows only on an open shape, not a placed one.
- **Source / Destination, bare spots** (owner review, 2026-11-26): the toggle was Marchers / Spots. While picking, the spots no longer show the drill number of whoever the option put there, since that doesn't matter yet. Swap two keeps them: there you click a spot for the marcher on it.
- **After simulated users** (3 sims, 2026-11-26, `~/ux-sim/who-goes-where/FINDINGS.md`):
  - The names moved to where the marchers start while picking: with none on the spots, nothing said who was who (2/3 hovered each dot to find out).
  - The floating editor keeps off the marchers where they start while picking: it sat on the front of the file and took clicks meant for them (3/3).
  - The faded side is less faded (0.4, was 0.2): the spots were invisible before the first pick (3/3; one read a pair that worked as a lost pick).
  - Source and Destination have tooltips (2/3 hovered them and got nothing).
  - Keep order's reason says drill numbers aren't an order (2/3 read their numbered file as one).
  - The Who goes where button says how many are placed by hand (3/3 thought their picks were gone when it still said Least travel).
  - Fixed: a hand pick that matched the spot the option already gave wasn't kept, so another option moved that marcher.
- **Descriptions and reasons in a tip** (owner review, 2026-11-26): the first build printed a description under Inside out, Zipper and Within sections, and each reason under its option. Now every option, including Least travel, Keep order and Reverse order (new descriptions), says what it does in a tip beside its row, with an (i) at the row's end; one that doesn't fit has a ? instead, and its tip adds why. The same way the timeline menus show an unavailable entry's reason. The tip opens on hover, a press, or the arrow keys, not when the menu opens.

## Not in this change

- Different count structures per marcher (staggered starts, holds inside the move).
- Picking by hand and Swap on a placed shape. They work while a shape is open in the shape tool.
  A placed shape keeps everyone's spot when it is reshaped; picking Least travel, Keep order or
  Reverse on it works it out again, as before.
- Translations other than English (the translation agent fills them later).

## Saved data

A placed shape already saves who goes where as `order_mode` (free text) and `reverse`, and each
member's spot as its slot. The new options save `order_mode` as `inside`, `zipper` or `sections`.
Hand picks and swaps are saved only as the members' slots: the shape remembers where everyone
stands, not how they were picked. An older build reads an unknown `order_mode` as Least travel
when it next works the shape out; until then everyone keeps their saved spot.
