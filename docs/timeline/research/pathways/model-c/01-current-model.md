## 3. How the resolver turns rows into positions

- **Spans** (`spec R-2/R-3`, lines 630–671): per marcher, rows are flattened by layer. A span is **founding** iff `s.start = T.start_beat`; otherwise it's a **join** (the row's first span) or a **resume**.
- **Progress**: `p = (b − s.start)/(T.end − s.start)` (`core/resolver.ts:535–536`). It is measured to the **transition's** end, not the row's end.
- **Dispatch**: `evalSpan` (`resolver.ts:538–565`) calls `walkedPoint` (`core/geom.ts:694–723`). For route style:
  - **not founding → `lerp(A, B, p)`, straight** (`geom.ts:706`; spec R-14 "Joins and resumes walk straight").
  - Own path (`params.offset` absent): `routePath(A, points, B, route)`, then `routePoint` at constant speed over the whole cleaned path. `A = origin(s)` is the marcher's **real start**; `from`/`to` are only tangent neighbours (spec R-14).
  - Group member: `groupPoint(groupMemberWalk(groupSchedule(route), A, B, offset), p)` (`geom.ts:708–717`; `groupSchedule` :568–573; `groupMemberWalk` :624–657; `groupPoint` :668–).
- **Offsets are measured from `route.from`.** That is F, the **mean of the members' real starts at draw time** (AP-R8, `timelineGroupRoutes.ts:240–242`), so `o_m = A_m − F`, stored as a constant vector. Rejoin uses `A_j − R.from` (:563). The offset is **not** recomputed from a live centroid. Lane = reference walk W (from F to G through the points) translated by o. Members:
  - walk a first leg from their own A to `W_1 + o` over `[0, u_first)`;
  - are rigid on the lanes over `[u_first, u_last]`;
  - walk a last leg from `W_m + o` to their own B. `u_first`/`u_last` are fixed by the route's lengths alone (spec R-14 group schedule items 3–7).
- **Inputs and caching**: a member reads only `origin(s)`, its destination, the route and its own offset, **never other members** (spec R-14 item 9). There is no centroid dependency, so invalidation is unchanged. Caches:
  - `routeGeo` per route (`resolver.ts:305, 365–386`), recomputed on a route change. W-3 is seeded for every walker via `routeWalkers` (:282–297, :812–817).
  - A `route_id` change goes through `setRoute` (:789–790).
  - The cleaned path, `groupSchedule` and the legs are **not cached**. They are rebuilt on every evaluation (`resolver.ts:13–15`; `geom.ts:708–717` calls `groupSchedule(route)` per point). That is correct (W-5 holds trivially) but costs O(points·K) per marcher per frame. I didn't measure it.
- **Destinations**: `dests[t][slot]`, from the shape or from the points (R-13). A route transition's one slot is its point.

### "Also test" items

- **Follow the leader on trails: no.** FTL requires a shape (`timeline_transitions_ftl_shape_check`), and E-R2 forbids a route on non-route styles (`triggers.ts:125–129`).
- **Staggers and ripples: no.** Every member has the same `p` at every beat (I-R5 plus founding at `T.start`, R-14 item 3). There's no per-member time offset.
- **Across page flags:** a group path lives in one move (I-R5). Basic "+ page flag" leaves route marchers alone, so the path crosses the flag. Split at the playhead refuses group paths.
- **Mirror, copy and paste: routes are never copied or mirrored.** Only end spots change.
- **Re-fit after start or end edits: none** (AP-R4/R5). Offsets stay frozen. Re-fit is step 3.
- **Basic and Expert:** Basic can't touch route marchers (`basicSimple.ts:68`).
- **Undo:** one transaction per action; rowids kept.
- **v0.1.7 files:** version 10 is written only on the first route (`electron/database/fileVersion.ts:19–22`). Migration 0031 adds NULL `route_id` (line 47).
- **Ownership:** a route has no owner column. E-R5 pins it to one move. Paths aren't standalone objects: deleting the last walker deletes the route (`timelineTransitionsInTransaction.ts:692–719`).

## 5. Cracks the brief doesn't mention

1. **I-A4 and I-A5 are timeless.** `UNIQUE(transition, slot)` and `UNIQUE(transition, marcher)` ignore beats (`schema.ts:516–523`). So no transition, route or not, can express "slot 3 is A until count 8, then B", or a marcher leaving and re-entering. Slot swaps, re-entry and "a marcher doesn't own its spot forever" need these constraints relaxed (to range-overlap triggers), which touches R-9 ("enough points because of I-A4", spec line 743). That is bigger than any route-table choice among A, B, B1 and B2.
2. **Joins never ride a path** (`geom.ts:706`), and progress is measured from the span start to `T.end` (`resolver.ts:535`). So any "enter later" or "re-enter" design also needs a resolver rule, not just storage.
3. **Breakout silently detaches from the destination shape and from the recipe.** `giveOwnTransition` writes a frozen point (:506–515). Recipes still list the marcher (no recipe code in the route files). The shape transition keeps vacant slots. Reshaping the shape later won't move path walkers.
4. **The offset origin is frozen at draw time** (`route.from = mean(A)`). After an earlier edit moves starts, first legs grow (AP-R5), and only "draw again" re-offsets. Rejoin after a point edit that rewrote `from` gives a different lane than before (spec AP-R10).
5. **A group path can shrink to one member silently.** Member removal goes through `removeAssignmentRowsInTransaction`/`clearOwnPageMoves` with no size check, while AP-R8 refuses fewer than 2 (`timelineGroupRoutes.ts:238`). The spec allows it (§5.2), but the UI's notion of a "group" then holds one marcher.
6. **Uncaught refusal (from reading, not tested):** `keepThroughMovesAcrossInTransaction` (`db/timelineNextPageStays.ts:157–167`) checks `splitRefusal` (no route reason), then calls `takeMarchersIntoOwnMoveInTransaction`, whose split throws `notYet` for a route member. The whole edit aborts instead of skipping that marcher.
7. **`takeMarchersIntoOwnMoveInTransaction` skips the route check** when the marchers are the whole membership (`timelineMoveCuts.ts:738`, sweep). That's harmless today because nothing is written.
8. **Casting and `removeAssignment` have no route awareness** (`timelineAssignmentEdits.ts`). They can leave a route walked by an empty transition, or cast a stranger onto a lane. They're unwired today, but would be live the moment casting gets a UI.
9. **The first row by `start_beat` is assumed** in `readMembers` (`timelineGroupRoutes.ts:100–109`), `drawRouteInTransaction` (`.get()`) and `ownPathInTransaction` (`:448–460`). A marcher with two rows in one move (different layers, which I-A3 allows) is resolved arbitrarily.
10. **Group geometry is recomputed per evaluation** (`groupSchedule` inside `walkedPoint`, `geom.ts:708–717`). There's no per-route cache of W or the moments, although spec W-5 permits one. This is a possible playback cost at large N; not measured.
11. **Model B's cost is visible in the schema:** the I-R2 CHECK (`slot_count = 1`, no shape) is a table CHECK. Lifting it needs a table rebuild migration, like 0031, plus an E-R4 rewrite, since "own vs group" is currently inferred from NULL vs offset with no flag.
12. **Split at the playhead's "before" half drops shapes, FTL and params** (`writeFirstHalfTransition`, `timelineSplitAtBeat.ts:553`, sweep). That's a precedent for lossy splits, and the reason AP-R7 needed a special route cut. Group paths have no exact cut at all (spec AP-R7, step 15).

Not found / not verified: the behaviour of recipe reopen on a route member; any UI caller of casting or recast; runtime cost of the per-frame `groupSchedule`.
