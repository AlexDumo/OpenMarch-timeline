A shape that changes, with marchers following around it design prototype

A drill-design idea: on page 1 the band stands in a bean, and by page 2 the bean has turned and flattened while everyone walks around it, follow-the-leader style. Two things stacked: the shape (its outline on page 1 and on page 2, blended between) and the follow (how far each marcher has walked around it). A diamond on the Shape lane marks a page that has its own shape. A page without one carries the shape from before it. Drag a shape on the field, or use the sliders, and press Play.
