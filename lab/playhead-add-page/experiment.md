The **+** that sat next to the playhead is gone. It looked like it did
something to the stretch between the start flag and the playhead, but it only
ever added one page flag where the playhead was. Adding a page now lives in
the playhead's right-click menu. Open a demo show to try it, in Basic or
Expert.

**First, before reading on:** try to add a new page somewhere in the
middle of the show without help. Note how you looked for it.

**What to try**

- Click inside a page to move the playhead there, then right-click the
  playhead (the line with the triangle on top). Choose **Create page at playhead**, or press **F**. A new
  page flag appears at the playhead and the new page is selected.
- Do it again and choose **Create subset at playhead** (Shift+F). The new page gets a letter
  ("2A") and later pages keep their numbers. Each entry shows the name the
  new page will get.
- Right-click the playhead while it sits on a page flag, or while playing.
  There's no menu there, since there's no page to add.
- Scroll past the last page, click there, and add a page from the playhead's
  menu. Then compare with the **+ N counts** button just after the last flag.

**Good to know**

- Moving marchers still uses the stretch between the start flag and the
  playhead. Only adding pages moved.
