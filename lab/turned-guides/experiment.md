Blocks aren't always square to the field. A box turned 15° or 30° can now be
lined up with the field the same way a square one can.

**New in this build:**

- **Moving a turned box:** when one of its **corners** or its **center** comes
  within a few pixels of a yard line, a hash or a sideline, the box lands with
  that point on the line. The orange line runs across the screen with its name
  ("S1: On 40", "On FH"), and an **orange ring** marks the corner on it.
- **Corner near two lines:** it lands right where they cross, with both guides.
- **Stretching a turned box:** drag a side or corner handle. A corner it moves
  lands on a nearby line, the opposite side stays put, and the spacing becomes
  whatever that takes.
- Square boxes work as before. A side on a line still turns thick and orange.
- Away from lines, a turned box still moves by half steps.

**What to try**

- Draw a box, turn it 15° with the knob, and drag it so its front corner sits
  on the 40.
- Put a corner of a turned box where a yard line crosses the front hash.
- Center a turned box on the 50.
- Stretch a turned box's side handle until a corner reaches a yard line.

**Good to know**

- Holding **Alt** moves and stretches freely, with no pull.
- A turned box has more points that can catch (four corners and a center), so
  it may catch more often than a square box. Tell us if it feels sticky.
- The pull reaches up to a quarter step while moving, so at a far zoom it's
  small. Zoom in for a stronger pull.
