The **View** tab in the toolbar is shorter. It keeps the toggles you flip
while working:

- **Labels** (drill numbers, numbers only, off)
- **Next paths**
- **Shapes** (when a placed box shows its outline)
- **Open 3D View**

The rest are set once, so they moved to **Settings › Field**: grid lines,
half lines, label size, label position, step-size warnings and **While
shaping** (who fades while a shape is open).

**What to try**

- Open the **View** tab and look over what's left.
- Open **Settings** (File › App Settings) and pick **Field**. Turn grid
  lines off with Settings still open and watch the field.
- Select a few marchers, press **B** to open a box, then switch **While
  shaping** between _Fade the rest_ and _Fade only while drawing_.
- Search Settings for "grid" or "label".

**Good to know**

- Your current choices carry over; nothing changes except where the
  controls live.
