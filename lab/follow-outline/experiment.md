**What's new**

- **Go round a circle or a box outline.** Select marchers standing all the way round a
  circle or the outline of a box (the Circle tool, or the Box tool with Outline) and press
  **Shift+L** (or the Follow button by the Path pen). The Follow tool opens in **Go round**:
  the outline is drawn with arrows showing the way round, and the marchers stand where
  they'll stop.
- **How far:** a number of **spots** (the places round the outline) or **steps**, with
  **−** and **+** or typed. It starts at what a normal step a count covers in the move.
  It can be more than once round; the bar then says how many times round.
- **Which way:** clockwise as the field is shown, unless the line has an order the show
  remembers that runs the other way. **Clockwise | Counterclockwise** on the bar, or
  **Reverse leader** (Alt+L), turns it round; the one at the back then leads.
- **Everyone goes the same distance,** so nobody passes anyone and a gap goes round with
  the line. The one behind the widest gap leads.
- **▶ Preview** loops the walk round. **Enter** (or Finish) writes it in one undo step; the
  next page's spots are where they stop.
- **A line standing on part of a circle** (an arc) opens as before, dragging a trail out;
  **Go round** on the bar takes it round the rest of that circle.
- **After finishing:** Reverse leader goes the other way round as far. **Shift+L** on the
  same line reopens Go round with its settings, to change how far. Remove trail walks
  everyone straight to the same spots.

**Try it**

1. On page 1, select eight marchers and draw a circle with the Circle tool (O).
2. Go to page 2, select the eight, press **Shift+L**.
3. Press **+** a few times, then **Clockwise** to go the other way, then **Preview**.
4. Press **Enter**, then **Space** to watch it.
5. Do the same with twelve marchers on a box outline (Box tool, then Outline).
6. Try it with a finger or pencil on a tablet: every choice is a button on the bar.

**Good to know**

- Go round needs four or more marchers all the way round the outline (or three or more on
  part of a circle). It says why before anything is drawn.
- The outline is fitted from where they stand. If you reshape the circle on page 1
  afterwards, the move still goes round the circle as it was, and anyone off it walks
  straight on first.

**Not in this step, on purpose**

- **Snakes through a box** (step 11).
- **Drawing your own closed outline** (a block letter) with the pen.
- Reading the placed shape itself instead of fitting one, so the move follows the shape
  when it's reshaped.
- A line on part of a box outline: a line alone doesn't say where the box is.
- Editing the outline's points. Reverse leader and Shift+L are the ways to change it.
- Turning counts at a box outline's corners (step 6's chips are for open trails).
