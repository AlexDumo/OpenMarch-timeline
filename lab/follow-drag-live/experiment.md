**What's new**

- **Arrows show where to grab.** When the Follow tool opens (Shift+L), each end of the
  line gets a ring and small arrows that pull outward along the line. The marchers in
  the middle don't take a drag. Point at one and a note says to drag from an end.
  Once you grab an end the arrows go.
- **A quieter field.** While the tool is open, everyone outside the line fades and
  can't be picked, as with the shape tools.
- **The line shows once.** Before you draw, the line's marchers stand at the start
  flag, where you grab them, instead of showing twice.
- **Marchers follow the pointer.** While you drag out the first leg, and between clicks
  after that, the marchers and their end spots move with the pointer, not only when you
  click. Preview walks the trail you're pointing at too.
- **What's saved is what you placed.** Finishing saves the points you clicked, not
  where the pointer last was.

**Good to know**

- Both ways of starting still work: press an end and drag out the first leg, or click
  an end, then move and click.
- With your computer set to reduce motion, the arrows stand still.
