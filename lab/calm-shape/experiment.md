An open shape now gets out of the way while you look at it. Select some
marchers, press **B** and drag a box.

**New in this build** (from your feedback on the last one):

- The spots are **solid purple dots**, so the shape you'll end up with is
  the strongest thing on the field. The hollow rings are where marchers
  start.
- The box's **purple fill shows only on hover**, with the handles.
- **Pathways start off.** Turn them on with the **Pathways** button in the
  floating editor under the shape. The choice is remembered.

**What to try**

- Move the pointer off the box. The fill, handles, rotate knob, center dot
  and gap ring fade, so the spots read clearly. Move back onto the box or a
  handle and they're back at full strength.
- Click **Pathways** in the floating editor to show the lines from the start
  flag and the rings where marchers start. Click again to hide them.
- Try it on an **Outline** box with an odd count: the gap's dashed ring
  fades with the other controls.

**Good to know**

- A placed box you select again doesn't fade yet; this build is only about
  the shape you're drawing.
