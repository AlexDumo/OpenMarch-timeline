An open shape now gets out of the way while you look at it. Select some
marchers, press **B** and drag a box.

**What to try**

- Move the pointer off the box. Its handles, rotate knob, center dot and
  gap ring fade, so the spots read clearly. Move back onto the box or a
  handle and they're back at full strength.
- Press **N**, or click **Pathways** next to the transport's speed button,
  to hide the lines from the start flag and the rings where marchers start.
  Press it again to bring them back. The choice is remembered.
- Try it on an **Outline** box with an odd count: the gap's dashed ring
  fades with the other controls.

**Good to know**

- N is the previous paths shortcut. It hides the open shape's lines while a
  shape is open, and is previous paths again once the shape is placed or
  discarded.
- A placed box you select again doesn't fade yet; this build is only about
  the shape you're drawing.
