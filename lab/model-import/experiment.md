**You'll need a 3D model file** (`.glb`, or `.gltf` with its files). If you
don't have one, free ones are on [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza) (pick a model,
then **Download → GLB**) or on Sketchfab (filter by **Downloadable**). Check
each model's license before using it in a real show.

**What to try**

- Open **Props** and select **Import…** under **Imported**, then pick your
  file. You can also drop the file on the editor.
- On the review screen:
  - Check that the picture shows the model's front facing you, right side up.
    If it doesn't, use **Rotate X**, **Rotate Y** and **Rotate Z**. Point at
    each one to see which way it turns.
  - Check the size. If it's far off, pick the unit the file was made in.
  - For a detailed model, try **Light**, **Detailed** and **Original** under
    **Detail**, and open **Compare in 3D…** to see what simplifying changed.
    **From the stands** shows it from about where the audience sits.
- Select **Add to show**, then place it from its tile under **Imported**.
  Place a few, resize and turn them on the field, and open the 3D View.
- Open **Manage assets** under **Imported** to see the show's models, their
  sizes and credits.
