OpenMarch is moving from pages to a **timeline**. This build is the timeline
as it is today. Some of it may not make sense until you try it, which is
exactly what we want to hear about.

Open a demo show to start. Older shows are converted to the timeline when
they open.

**1. The start flag (yellow line)**

The yellow vertical line on the timeline is the **start flag**. It marks where
the marchers are coming from. When you move marchers, the transition you're
editing runs from the start flag to the playhead.

**2. Loop a transition while you edit it**

Press **C**, or the **Loop** button, to loop the selected part of the
timeline. Edit marchers while it loops to see the result straight away. Press
**C** again to stop looping.

**3. Your own moves, anywhere in the show**

Hold **Ctrl** (**⌘** on a Mac) and drag across the timeline to select any
stretch of time: part of a page, or across several pages. Then drag a
marcher to make a move over exactly that stretch.

**4. Double-click to see the real moves**

Double-click a transition or a page to see the moves inside it. For example,
a line of marchers can step forward for a page while a few fan out at the
end. Those are two separate moves that you can edit on their own. The
fan-out starts from wherever the main move puts those marchers, so if you
change the main move, the fan-out follows.

**5. Holds carry across pages**

If pages 2, 3 and 4 are a hold, editing page 2 also changes pages 3 and 4.
You can unlink them so a page stands on its own. That part is the newest, so
tell us how it behaves.

**What to try**

- Pick a page, move a few marchers, and press **C** to watch the transition
  loop.
- Ctrl-drag (⌘-drag) across part of a page, or across two pages, and drag a
  marcher to make a move just there.
- Double-click a page that has a move inside it and edit one of its moves.
- Find a hold that lasts several pages, edit the first page, and step
  through the rest.

**Good to know**

- This runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and reloading the tab
  loses unsaved changes to your show. Use **Download .dots** to keep a copy.
- It's a work in progress: rough edges and bugs are expected. Tell us about
  them.
