This build has **two versions of Play**. Switch between them any time with
**Version: A / B** at the bottom left. Your show stays as it is when you
switch, so you can try the same thing both ways.

**Version A (what the app does now)**

- **Space** plays on from the **playhead** and stops where you stop it.
- **Shift+Space** plays the selected page's move once, then goes back.

**Version B**

- **Space** (the Play button) plays on from the **yellow start flag**.
- **Shift+Space** (a second Play button, next to Play) plays on from the
  **playhead**.

In both versions, looping (**C** or **Loop**) works the same: Space loops the
yellow bar.

**What to try**

- Open a demo show. Click a page in the timeline, then press Space. Try the
  same in both versions.
- Drag the playhead to the middle of a page and play from there.
- Edit a few marchers on a page, then play to check your work.
- Turn on looping with **C**, play, then turn it off.

**Good to know**

- This runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and reloading the tab
  loses unsaved changes to your show.
- The feedback you send records which version was on at the time; say which
  one you mean in your answers anyway.
