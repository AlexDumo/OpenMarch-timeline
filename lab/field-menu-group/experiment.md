Right-click the field, then right-click a page's box on the timeline. Both menus
now have the same group of page commands.

**What to try**

- Select a few marchers and right-click the field. Try **Define as shown** and
  **Hold from previous page**.
- Keep them selected and right-click the box of a page that isn't the one you're
  on. The heading names that page, and the commands change that page.
- Try **Copy positions from next page** from either menu.
- Select nobody and right-click empty field or a page's box. The heading says
  **All performers on Page N**, and the item reads **Clear everyone from Page N**.
  It turns red when you point at it.

**Good to know**

- The field's menu works on the page at the playhead.
- Undo brings back anything you clear.
