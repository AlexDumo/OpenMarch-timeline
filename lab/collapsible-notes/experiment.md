The **Notes** box at the bottom of the inspector (right side) takes room even
when you aren't writing notes. In this build you can fold it away.

**What to try**

- Open a demo show and click **Notes** at the bottom of the inspector to
  collapse it, then again to open it.
- Write a note on one page, collapse notes, and step through the pages.
  A small dot next to **Notes** means the page has a note.
- Reload the tab and open a show again: notes should stay the way you left
  them.

**Good to know**

- This runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and reloading the tab
  loses unsaved changes to your show.
