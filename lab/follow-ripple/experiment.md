**What's new**

- **Ends: Ripple in.** In the Follow tool (**Shift+L**), when the line already has end spots
  on this page and they stand in a line, curve or arc, the bar's Ends has a third choice
  beside From trail and Keep end spots: **Ripple in**. You can pick it before grabbing an end.
- **Drag from an end onto the near end of the new form and let go.** The leader walks
  straight there and then along the form; everyone follows through the same corner. It's
  one follow move and one undo step.
- **Corners first if you like.** Click for corners before reaching the form; a click on the
  form's end, a double-click, **Enter** or **Finish** ends it. With nothing drawn, Finish
  gives the straight way in.
- **Who stands where.** The last marcher takes the end the line enters and the leader the
  far end, each in order between, so nobody passes anyone. If the form was placed with a
  shape tool, it's updated too, so it opens in the shape tools with everyone on a spot.
- **The leader's count into the form** shows on the bar. Make the page longer or the way in
  shorter to change it.
- **▶ Preview** loops the walk; Big steps checks each marcher's whole way.
- **Keep end spots** can now also finish with nothing drawn: a straight way into the spots
  each marcher already has.

**Try it**

1. Select five marchers in a line and drag them 8 steps up on the next page.
2. Press **Shift+L**, then **Ripple in** on the bar.
3. Drag from the left end of the line onto the left end of the raised line and let go.
4. Press **Shift+Space** to play the page. Then undo and try dragging toward the other end.
5. Try it with a finger or pencil on a tablet: Ends, Preview and Finish are all buttons.

**Good to know**

- Ripple in is greyed out when the end spots are scattered, close into a loop or fill a
  block (a box or a circle), or lie on the line itself. Press it anyway and it says why (a
  tooltip with a mouse, a message with a finger). With no end spots on the page it says that
  placing them first unlocks it.
- The line is read where its move starts, so a window starting on an earlier page still
  ripples from where the line stands when this move begins.
- Enter or Finish writes the deal you see: if the drawing shows the far end, that's the end
  the line enters.
- After a ripple it's an ordinary follow move: drag its points, Reverse leader, Remove
  trail, resize the page; split at the playhead still says no, as for any trail.

**Not in this step, on purpose**

- Rippling into a box, a circle or an outline (snake order and outlines come in steps 10
  and 11).
- A form with more spots than the line, or rippling into another page's trail.
- Typing a count for the straight piece (that would be a pin, step 5).
- Following the true curve of the form the line starts in (step 10).
- Corner counts on the way in (step 6).
