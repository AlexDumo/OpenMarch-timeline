<InShort>Select <Ui k="fieldDisplay.overlays" /> on the field's display bar (top right of the field) and turn on <Ui k="fieldDisplay.figures" />.</InShort>

The 2D field can draw each performer as a figure: a small picture of a performer seen from straight above, holding their section's instrument, at its real size on the field. Tubas, bass drums and flags take up the room they do on a real field, so you can see whether a form has space for them. Figures are off until you turn them on, and the choice is kept on this computer, not in the show.

## Show figures instead of dots

<Steps>

1. On the display bar at the top right of the field, select <Ui k="fieldDisplay.overlays" /> (the eye).

2. Turn on <Ui k="fieldDisplay.figures" />.

3. Beside the switch, choose <Ui k="fieldDisplay.figuresAlone" /> for figures only, or <Ui k="fieldDisplay.figuresWithDots" /> to draw each performer's dot on top of their figure.

</Steps>

Figures show at every zoom. Zoomed out to the whole field they're small, but you can still tell a tuba from a trumpet; zoom in to see them in detail.

To turn figures on or off without the panel, use View › <Ui k="fieldDisplay.figures" plain /> in the app menu, or <Ui k="actions.ui.showPerformerFigures" /> and <Ui k="actions.ui.hidePerformerFigures" /> in the command palette.

## What the figures show

Each figure is made from the 3D View's own models, so it shows what the 3D View shows for that performer: their section's instrument, held as they stand at attention. It follows the show's 3D View settings ([Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/#choose-the-horns)):

- <Ui k="view3d.settings.tuba" />: a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.contra" plain /> or a <Ui k="view3d.tuba.sousaphone" plain />, whichever the show uses.
- <Ui k="view3d.settings.brassFinish" />: brass horns in gold or silver.
- Bass drums: each bass drum player carries their own drum size, from smallest to largest, as in the 3D View.

Each figure wears the show's uniform, as the 3D View dresses it ([Change the venue and uniforms](/guides/3d-view/venue-and-uniforms/)), and drum shells and flag silks take their section's color. Everyone has the same body, so what you compare between figures is what they carry. Until a show's figures are made, the field shows ready-made grey ones.

Figures don't yet change with Equipment cues (someone handed a flag still shows their own instrument), and they all face the front, whichever way the performers face.

## When a show's figures are made

OpenMarch makes the figures a show needs in the background, the first time you turn figures on for that show, and again after a setting that changes them, such as <Ui k="view3d.settings.tuba" plain />. Until they're ready, the field shows a ready-made set; you can keep working the whole time. The show keeps its figures, so the next time you open it they're there at once, on this computer or another one.
