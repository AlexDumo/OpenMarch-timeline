<InShort>In the 3D View, drag to pan, drag with the right mouse button to orbit, and scroll to zoom. Press <Kbd keys="1" /> to <Kbd keys="9" /> to jump to a camera.</InShort>

You can look at the show from anywhere: move the view with the mouse, jump to one of the venue's cameras, or sit in a seat in the stands. Cameras you add or change are saved with the show.

## Move the view

These are the default controls, called <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.map" plain />:

| Do this                                                                                                   | To                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Drag                                                                                                      | Pan across the field               |
| Drag with the right mouse button, or hold <Kbd keys="Alt" />, <Kbd keys="Ctrl" /> or <Kbd keys="Shift" /> while dragging | Orbit around the field             |
| Scroll or pinch                                                                                           | Zoom toward the pointer            |
| Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" /> or <Kbd keys="Alt" /> while scrolling                                           | Pan                                |
| Double-click a spot on the field                                                                          | Orbit around that spot from now on |

<Media
  id="3d-move-view"
  alt="The 3D View with the default controls: a drag pans across the field, a drag with the right mouse button orbits the camera, and the mouse wheel zooms in toward the band."
/>

The camera never goes below the ground. As soon as you move the view, the camera menu reads <Ui k="view3d.overlay.freeCamera" plain />; choose a camera to go back to a set position.

To drag to orbit instead, open <Ui k="view3d.settings.title" /> (the gear) in the 3D View and choose <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.orbit" plain />, or <Ui k="settings.view3d.controls.trackpad" plain /> if you use a trackpad. [Settings](/guides/reference/settings/#3d-view) says what each one does.

On a touch screen, one finger orbits and two fingers pan and zoom, whichever controls you choose ([Use OpenMarch on a tablet](/guides/workspace/on-a-tablet/)).

## Go to a camera

Every venue comes with its own cameras, such as the press box, the front row and a view from straight above.

<Steps>

1. In the 3D View, select the camera menu at the top left. It shows the name of the camera you're on.

2. Choose a camera. The view flies there.

</Steps>

Or press <Kbd keys="1" /> to <Kbd keys="9" /> for the first nine cameras in the menu. Choosing the camera you're already on brings the view back to it after you've moved away.

## Sit in the stands

In a venue with stands, you can watch from any seat.

<Steps>

1. Select <Ui k="view3d.overlay.pickSeat" /> (the armchair next to the camera menu).

2. Click a seat in the stands. The view flies there and looks at the field from that seat's height.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-pick-seat"
  alt="Pick a seat is selected next to the camera menu, then clicking a seat in the visitors' stands across the field flies the view there, looking back at the band."
/>

Press <Kbd keys="Escape" /> to stop picking without moving. Spectators around your seat step aside so they don't block the view.

## Save your own cameras

The cameras are a list you can change. It's saved in the show, one list for each venue, and the 3D View opens on the first camera in it. Changes are saved straight away, and undo in the editor doesn't undo them.

<Steps>

1. Select the camera menu, then <Ui k="view3d.cameras.edit" />. The <Ui k="view3d.cameras.title" plain /> panel opens under the menu.

2. Move the view to where you want a camera.

3. Select <Ui k="view3d.cameras.addView" />. A new camera is added at the end of the list, with its name ready to type.

4. Type a name and press <Kbd keys="Enter" />.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="3d-cameras-panel"
  alt="The Cameras panel under the camera menu: a row for each camera with its number, name and buttons to set it to the current view, move it up or down, or remove it, and Add current view and Reset to defaults at the bottom."
  maxWidth={480}
/>

Each row starts with the camera's number key: select it to fly there. Then comes the name, which you can change in place, and these buttons:

- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.setView" />: moves the camera to where the view is now.
- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.moveUp" /> and <Ui k="view3d.cameras.moveDown" />: change the order, and with it the number keys. The camera at the top is the one the 3D View opens on.
- <Ui k="view3d.cameras.remove" />: removes the camera. Select <Ui k="view3d.cameras.undo" /> in the panel within a few seconds to bring it back.

A venue can have up to 30 cameras. <Ui k="view3d.cameras.reset" /> replaces your list with the venue's own cameras; if you've changed the list, it asks first, because your list isn't kept anywhere else.
