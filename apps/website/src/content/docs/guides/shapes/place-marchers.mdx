<InShort>Click any marcher in the shape to select the whole shape, then drag its handles or change its numbers in the floating editor. Each change saves when you let go.</InShort>

A placed shape keeps its kind and its numbers, so you can come back and change it as a shape rather than marcher by marcher. To draw one, see [How shapes work](/guides/shapes/).

## Change a placed shape

Selecting a placed shape brings back its outline, handles and <Term id="floating-editor">floating editor</Term>, with no Place button. Each change is one step you can undo, and undo or redo selects the shape it brings back, all of it.

  alt="A placed box of trumpets. Clicking one trumpet selects the whole box, showing its outline, handles and floating editor; the box's right side handle is dragged out, the trumpets spread wider, and the change is saved when the button is let go."
/>

Drag inside the shape, or press the arrow keys, to move it. Its handles and numbers work as they did before you placed it; see [Draw a box](/guides/shapes/draw-a-box/#change-the-boxs-numbers) and the other draw pages.

## Link lines, curves and arcs end to end

Placed lines, curves and arcs on the same page can be linked at their ends, like the stem and hook of a J, and still stay separate shapes: each keeps its own marchers and its own interval, and clicking one selects only that one.

To link two, select one and drag one of its ends (or the whole shape) onto an end of the other. While you drag an end, the readout by the pointer says <Ui k="shapes.links.dropTip" plain />. When the pointer comes near the other shape's end, a ring shows on that end and the readout says <Ui k="shapes.links.releaseTip" values={{ shape: "Line" }} plain />; let go and your end lands exactly there and attaches. An arc's end goes there too, the arc turning and growing about its other end, though dragging its end otherwise only stretches its width. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" /> while you drag to put the end there without attaching.

You can link a new line, curve or arc as you draw it, too. Start the drag that draws it on a placed shape's end, or end the drag there, and the new shape's end lands exactly on that end, with the same ring and readout. Drawing a line or curve by clicking its points links the same way: when the pointer comes near a placed shape's end, a ring shows on it and the readout says <Ui k="shapes.links.clickTip" values={{ shape: "Line" }} plain />. Click the first point there, or the last point before you finish the line, and that end lands exactly on it and links. A point you click there in the middle of the line doesn't link, and a line you close into a loop has no ends to link. Before you select <Ui k="shapes.edit.place" />, you can also drag one of the new shape's end handles onto a placed shape's end. Placing it saves the link, as one step you can undo. Hold <Kbd keys="Mod" /> while you draw or click to put the end there without linking.

One marcher stands on the joint, the one that was already on the end you dragged onto. The shape you dragged (or drew) holds none there: its first marcher stands one of its own intervals from the joint. While either shape is selected, a small ring with a diamond marks the joint; with nothing selected, linked shapes look like any others.

After that, moving or changing either shape moves the other's end at the joint with it. That shape stretches, its far end staying where it is, and its marchers spread out again along it. Dragging the end at the joint moves both ends. It's all one step you can undo.

Shapes linked together (a J and the line after it, say) are selected together: clicking any of them selects them all, every marcher of each. Drag them, or press the arrow keys, to move them all as one; their links hold. To pick one of the shapes, click it again, or double-click it; from there, clicking another of the linked shapes picks that one, and <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> selects them all again.

Right-clicking the joint of the selected shape and choosing <Ui k="actions.shape.selectLinked" /> selects them all too; it's in the command palette too, and in the field's menu while a linked shape is selected. On a tablet, long-press the joint or a marcher of the shape and choose it there.

To take them apart, right-click the joint of the selected shape (or long-press it on a tablet) and choose <Ui k="shapes.links.detachFrom" values={{ shape: "Line" }} /> (it names the shape on the other side of the joint); it's in the command palette too, and in the field's menu while a linked shape is selected. The shape you dragged gets a marcher on its own end again, standing on the same spot as the other shape's until you drag one of them away.

A link holds only on the page the two shapes were placed on: changing one on a later page gives that page its own copy, not linked. Ungrouping either shape ends the link too. Boxes and circles can't be linked.

## Select one marcher in a shape

With the shape selected, click a marcher in it again, or double-click it, to select that marcher alone. On linked shapes it takes one more: the first picks the shape, the next the marcher. <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" /> does it in one click, and so does right-clicking the marcher and choosing <Ui k="actions.shape.enterBox" />. Press <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" /> to go back to the whole shape.

## Take a marcher out of a shape

Move a marcher off their spot and they leave the shape; a notice says so, and their spot stays empty. In a closed shape, <Ui k="shapes.box.left.closeUp" /> in that notice, or in the floating editor while the shape is selected, lays the marchers left out in the shape again: a box in Grid takes the layout that fits the new count, or Short back, and an outline spreads round again. A box also offers <Ui k="shapes.box.left.resplit" />, which lays it out again for the marchers left. Undo puts the marcher back.

## Ungroup a shape

To stop treating the marchers as a shape, select it and press <Kbd action="shapeUngroup" />, or right-click it and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.ungroup" />. Nobody moves.

## Make a shape from where marchers stand

Select the marchers, right-click one of them and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.group" /> (<Kbd action="shapeGroup" />). Nobody moves. From then on, clicking any of them selects them all, and you can move, turn, flip and scale them together with the shape's handles and floating editor.

## Shapes on later pages

On the pages after, the shape is there too for as long as every one of its marchers stays on its spot, and they're <Term id="held">Held</Term> there: the shape is their hold. You can select it on those pages. Changing it on a later page gives that page its own copy of the shape; the earlier page keeps the one it had.

## Marchers with a position in the middle of the page (Basic)

In Basic, a shape placed at a page's flag holds only the marchers whose last [position in the middle of the page](/guides/timeline/moves/#set-a-position-in-the-middle-of-a-page-basic) is at the same count. Marchers whose last one is at another count still go to their spots, but aren't part of the shape, and a notice names them. They won't move with the shape when you change it later. To keep everyone in it, take away the positions that differ, or give everyone theirs at the same count, then place the shape again.

The same notice appears when you later give marchers in a shape positions in the middle of the page at different counts: the shape keeps the marchers of one count, and names the others.
