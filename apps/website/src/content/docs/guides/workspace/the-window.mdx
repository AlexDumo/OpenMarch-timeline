When a show is open, the field fills the middle of the window. You arrange marchers on it, and the timeline under it holds the show's counts, pages and moves, so you can see and change when everything happens.

## The field

The field shows where every marcher is at the playhead's count. Select marchers by clicking them or dragging a box around them, and drag them to move them. The yard lines, hashes and grid come from the field's settings, which you can change to match the field you rehearse on.

To pan and zoom, see [Move around the field](/guides/workspace/move-around-the-field/).

## The timeline

The timeline runs along the bottom of the window. It has the playback controls, the pages of the show, its counts and measures, and the music's waveform. The playhead marks the count the field is showing: drag it, or play the show, and the marchers move.

Pages and moves are how you shape the drill over time. [How the timeline works](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/) explains them.

## The toolbar

The tabs across the top hold the tools for changing positions:

- <Ui k="inspector.shape.fileTab" />: open, save, export and print.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" />: snapping, locking movement to one axis, lining marchers up and spacing them evenly.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.shapesTab" />: drawing a box, line or arc and placing marchers in it.
- <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" />: selecting marchers by section or by what they're doing.

At the right end of the toolbar, <Ui k="view3d.open" /> opens the show in the 3D View, and the <Ui k="workspace.editor" /> and <Ui k="workspace.preview" /> switch changes between editing the show and watching it (<Kbd action="togglePreview" />).

## The sidebar

The icons down the left edge open panels for the show as a whole: its <Ui k="sidebar.marchers" />, <Ui k="sidebar.field" />, music and metronome, marchers' <Ui k="sidebar.appearance" />, and <Ui k="props.title" />. At the bottom are the <Ui k="sidebar.mobile" /> export and <Ui k="sidebar.settings" />, such as the number the first page starts at.

## The inspector

The inspector on the right shows the details of what you've selected: a page's counts and measures when nothing else is selected, or the selected marchers and their positions. Each page's notes are at the bottom.

## Finding a command

Every command in OpenMarch can be found by name. Select the search icon in the title bar, or press <Kbd action="openCommandPalette" />, and start typing what you want to do. The list shows each command's shortcut, so it's also the quickest way to learn them. All shortcuts are listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guides/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/), and the app shows them too with <Kbd action="showShortcuts" />.
