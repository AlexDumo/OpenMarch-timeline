OpenMarch's music system is built around **Tempo Groups** - collections of measures that share the same time signature and tempo. This system gives you precise control over your show's timing and supports everything from simple 4/4 marches to complex mixed meters like 7/8 or 5/8.

## Getting Started: The Complete Workflow

Here's the recommended order of operations for setting up music in your show:

<Steps>

1. **Import your audio file** (optional but recommended)
   - Click the Music button in the toolbar
   - In the Audio section, click "Import audio file"
   - Select your audio file (.mp3, .wav, etc.)
   - Give it a nickname for easy identification

2. **Create your first tempo group**
   - In the Tempo Groups section, click "Add New Group"
   - Fill out the form with your music's details:
     - **Name**: Rehearsal letter or section name (optional)
     - **Start Tempo**: The BPM for this section
     - **Beats per measure**: Time signature (4 for 4/4, 3 for 3/4, etc.)
     - **Number of measures**: How many measures this tempo/feel lasts

3. **Add additional tempo groups**
   - Continue adding groups for each section of your show
   - Each time the tempo, time signature, or feel changes, create a new group
   - Use the "+" button between existing groups to insert new sections

4. **Refine your timing**
   - Edit any tempo group by clicking the pencil icon
   - Add tempo changes (accelerando/ritardando) using the "End Tempo" field
   - For complex timing, you can set individual beat tempos

</Steps>

## Understanding Tempo Groups

### What is a Tempo Group?

A tempo group is a collection of consecutive measures that share:
- The same time signature
- The same basic tempo
- The same rhythmic feel

### When to Create a New Group

Create a new tempo group when:
- The tempo changes (even slightly)
- The time signature changes (4/4 to 3/4, etc.)
- You want to mark a new section with a rehearsal letter
- You need different rhythmic feels (regular vs. mixed meter)

### Group Names and Organization

- **Names are optional** but helpful for organization
- Use rehearsal letters ("A", "B", "Closer") or descriptive names
- Groups with the same tempo and time signature will be combined automatically unless the second group has a name

## Advanced Features

### Mixed Meter Support

OpenMarch supports complex time signatures like 7/8, 5/8, 10/8, etc.

<Steps>

1. When creating a tempo group, set your beats per measure to the total number of eighth notes
2. Click "Make mixed meter" to enable pattern selection
3. Choose your beat pattern (e.g., 3+2+2 for 7/8)
4. The system will automatically handle the long and short beats

</Steps>

**Common mixed meter patterns:**
- 7/8: 3+2+2, 2+3+2, or 2+2+3
- 5/8: 3+2 or 2+3
- 10/8: 3+2+3+2 or 2+3+2+3

### Tempo Changes (Accelerando/Ritardando)

For gradual tempo changes within a section:

<Steps>

1. Set your **Start Tempo** to the beginning tempo
2. Set your **End Tempo** to where you want the tempo to be heading
3. The system will create a smooth transition that approaches (but doesn't reach) the end tempo
4. This prepares for a smooth transition to your next tempo group

</Steps>

### Individual Beat Editing

For sections with irregular tempo fluctuations:

<Steps>

1. Create your tempo group normally
2. Click the pencil icon to edit the group
3. If the system detects tempo variations, you'll see individual beat tempo controls
4. Adjust each beat's tempo as needed

</Steps>

## Tips for Success

### Starting Your Show

1. **Begin with the audio file** - This gives you a reference for tempo and timing
2. **Work section by section** - Don't try to map your entire show at once
3. **Start simple** - Use basic tempo groups first, then add complexity
4. **Test and refine** - Use the timeline to check your timing against the audio

### Common Workflows

**For simple shows:**
- One tempo group per major section
- Use rehearsal letters for names
- Keep time signatures consistent

**For complex shows:**
- Multiple tempo groups per section
- Use end tempos for smooth transitions
- Take advantage of mixed meter support

### Troubleshooting

If you're having issues:
- **Refresh the page** (`Ctrl/Cmd + R`) - This often fixes display issues
- **Check your math** - Make sure your measures add up to your music
- **Start over** - Sometimes it's easier to delete and recreate a problematic group

## What's Different from Before

If you used OpenMarch's old music system:
- **No more MusicXML imports** - Everything is done with tempo groups
- **Much more control** - You can specify exactly what you want
- **Better mixed meter support** - Complex time signatures are now easy
- **Smoother workflow** - No more workarounds or limitations

The new system is designed to handle any type of music, from simple marches to complex contemporary pieces with changing meters and tempos.
