Most tools act on the selected marchers: moving, aligning, placing them on a shape, holding them on a page. Selected marchers are highlighted on the field, and the inspector lists their drill numbers.

## Click to select

- Click a marcher to select only that marcher, even if others were selected.
- <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> a marcher to add it to the selection, or to take it out if it's already selected.
- Click empty field to clear the selection, or press <Kbd action="cancelAlignmentUpdates" />.

## Drag a box

Drag on empty field to draw a box. When you let go, every marcher whose dot the box touches is selected, and the previous selection is cleared.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-box"
  alt="A box is dragged around a row of eight marchers, which become selected. Then a marcher in the row behind them is clicked with Shift held and joins the selection."
/>

- Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="drag" /> as you start the box to add to the selection.
- Hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> as you start the box to take the marchers it touches out of the selection.

A drag that starts inside the frame around several selected marchers moves them instead of drawing a box. Start the box outside the frame, or click empty field first to clear the selection.

## Draw a lasso

For a group that isn't a rectangle, such as a curved line or a block with marchers close around it, draw a lasso:

1. Double-click empty field, and hold the button down on the second click.
2. Drag around the marchers you want.
3. Bring the pointer back near where you started. The line turns green when letting go will close it.
4. Let go.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-lasso"
  alt="A double-click and drag on empty field draws a freehand line around a ring of marchers. The line closes back at its start, and every marcher inside it is selected."
/>

Every marcher whose dot is inside the lasso is selected. If you let go far from the start, the lasso selects nothing. <Kbd keys="Shift" /> and <Kbd keys="Alt" />, held as you start, add to and take away from the selection, as they do for a box.

## Select everyone

Press <Kbd action="selectAllMarchers" />, or right-click empty field and choose <Ui k="actions.select.selectAll" />. Props aren't included; select them on the field.

## Select by section, tag, family or drill prefix

The <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab in the toolbar selects groups of marchers by what they are rather than where they stand.

<Media
  id="marchers-select-tab"
  alt="The Select tab: a Select by menu set to Section, a button for each section in the show, and Select all."
/>

1. Select the <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab.
2. Open <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectByText" /> and choose <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.section" />, <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.tag" />, <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.family" /> or <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.drillPrefix" />.
3. Select a group to select all of its marchers.

Choosing a group replaces the selection. Hold <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> to add the group to it instead, for example to select the trumpets and the mellophones together.

- <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.family" /> groups sections by family: Woodwind, Brass, Battery, Guard, Other and Pit.
- <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectBy.tag" /> lists the tags you've made in the inspector. To make one, select marchers and use the tag buttons at the bottom of the inspector.
- With many groups, a search box appears after the first few. For sections, tags and drill prefixes, the ones you used last come first.

OpenMarch remembers which kind of group you chose. <Ui k="actions.select.selectAll" /> at the end of the tab selects every marcher.

## Shapes you've placed

Marchers placed on a box are selected together: clicking one of them selects the whole box. Double-click a marcher in it to select only that marcher, or <Kbd keys="Mod" gesture="click" /> it to reach inside the box straight away. You can also right-click a marcher in the selected box and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.enterBox" />. See [Shapes](/guides/shapes/).

## On a tablet

On a touch screen, tap a marcher to select only it. Your tap doesn't have to land exactly on the dot: the nearest marcher within about a fingertip's width is picked. Tap empty field to clear the selection. To add marchers to the selection, tap <Ui k="touch.modifiers.add" /> on the buttons over the field to keep it on, then tap each marcher.

Touch and hold a marcher, or empty field, with a finger or a pen, then lift without moving, to open the field's menu, as a right-click does. This works in every drag mode (below). In a placed shape that's selected, hold one of its marchers and choose <Ui k="actions.shape.enterBox" /> to select only that marcher.

What a drag on empty field does depends on the drag mode, the first button over the field (<Ui k="touch.dragMode.prefix" plain /> followed by the mode's name). Tap it to choose:

- <Ui k="touch.dragMode.auto" />, the starting mode: one finger moves the view, and a pen such as Apple Pencil draws a box. To draw a box with a finger, press and hold on empty field for half a second, then drag; if you lift without moving instead, the field's menu opens.
- <Ui k="touch.dragMode.box" />: one finger, or the pen, draws a box. Use two fingers to move the view.
- <Ui k="touch.dragMode.lasso" />: one finger, or the pen, draws around the marchers to select. Use two fingers to move the view.
- <Ui k="touch.dragMode.pan" />: one finger, or the pen, moves the view.

In every mode, a drag that starts on a marcher, or inside the frame around the selection, moves the marchers. The mode is kept on this device, not in the show file.

To take marchers out of the selection, tap <Ui k="touch.modifiers.subtract" /> to keep it on, then draw a box or lasso around them, as <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> does with a mouse. <Ui k="touch.modifiers.add" /> and <Ui k="touch.modifiers.subtract" /> don't stay on together: turning one on turns the other off.

The <Ui k="inspector.shape.selectTab" /> tab selects whole groups: there, a switch beside <Ui k="toolbar.select.selectByText" /> chooses whether a tapped group replaces the selection or adds to it.
