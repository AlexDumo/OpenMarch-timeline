<InShort>Select the marchers, right-click one and choose <Ui k="field.menu.facing" /> › a direction, or press <Kbd action="facingSide2" /> for side 2. To turn over the counts before, pull the tab on the left of its diamond in the Facing lane to the left. Click a diamond in the lane's top row to select who turns there.</InShort>

Every marcher faces the front until you turn them, except bass drums and flub drums, which start on side 2 with their right side toward the front. A facing change says where the selected marchers face by a count: they face that way on it, and until their next facing change. They can turn into it instantly or over the counts before it. The field can draw a small caret in front of each marcher's dot, its point aimed the way they face ([See facing on the field](#see-facing-on-the-field)), and the 3D View turns them.

## Turn marchers

<Steps>

1. Move the <Term id="playhead">playhead</Term> to the count where the turn starts.
2. Select the marchers.
3. Right-click one of them on the field and open <Ui k="field.menu.facing" />. On a tablet, press and hold a selected marcher to open the same menu.
4. Choose where they face from the playhead on.

</Steps>

The <Ui k="field.menu.facing" plain /> submenu has:

- <Ui k="cues.facing.front" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingFront" />), <Ui k="cues.facing.back" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingBack" />), <Ui k="cues.facing.side1" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingSide1" />) and <Ui k="cues.facing.side2" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingSide2" />). The sides use the field's side names, so a stage reads "Stage left" and "Stage right" ([Name the field and its sides](/guides/field/edit-the-field/#name-the-field-and-its-sides)). A direction everyone selected already faces is checked.
- <Ui k="cues.facing.turnLeft" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingTurnLeft" />) and <Ui k="cues.facing.turnRight" plain /> (<Kbd action="facingTurnRight" />): each marcher turns 45° to their own left or right from where they face.
- <Ui k="cues.facing.angle" plain />: type an angle in the command bar, then press Enter.

The command palette has all of them too. The marchers face the new way on the playhead's count, turning instantly until you give the turn counts (below). Each marcher turns from the way they face: picking <Ui k="cues.facing.back" plain /> turns someone facing the front half a turn and someone facing side 2 a quarter turn. Front, Back and the sides take the shorter way round; when both ways are half a turn, the marcher turns to their left.

Choosing a facing for marchers who already have a facing change on that count replaces theirs. Each change is one step of undo (<Kbd action="performUndo" />), and none works while the show plays.

## Angles

A facing is a number of degrees: 0° is the front (the audience), 90° is side 2, 180° is the back, and 270° (or −90°) is side 1. A larger angle turns to the marcher's left, as they face the front.

Angles don't wrap around, so they say how a marcher gets there:

- 360° is a full spin to the left, ending facing the front. −360° spins to the right.
- −270° ends facing side 2, like 90°, but gets there by turning three quarters to the right.
- After a spin, a marcher faces 360°. <Ui k="cues.facing.front" plain /> then leaves them where they are, at 360°, instead of spinning them back.

<Ui k="cues.facing.angle" plain /> turns everyone to exactly the angle you type, whatever they faced before.

## Turn over the counts before

The <Ui k="cues.lane.facing" plain /> lane on the timeline appears with the show's first facing change; to show or hide it, use the <Ui k="cues.lane.lanes" /> row ([Lanes](/guides/timeline/how-the-timeline-works/#lanes)). When you set a facing and the lane is below the bottom of the timeline, the timeline opens, or scrolls, far enough to show it.

The lane's top row has a diamond on each count where anyone's facing changes. When several groups change on the same count, even with turns of different lengths, they share one diamond, with a small number beside it saying how many; point at it to see who. With marchers selected, the lane adds a row for each group of them who turn alike, showing their turns; with nobody selected, it's only the row of diamonds.

- Click a diamond in the top row to select the marchers who change facing there. <Kbd keys="Shift" gesture="click" /> adds them to the selection, or takes them out if they're all selected already. On a tablet, turn on <Ui k="touch.modifiers.add" plain /> on the touch pill first to add.
- In a group's row, a diamond marks each of their facing changes on its count. The solid bar after it is the angle they hold from there, up to their next facing change.
- A pale bar with a dashed edge in front of the diamond is the turn into it. Its label says which way they turn, how far and over how many counts, such as ↺ 180° · 2 counts. ↺ is a turn to the marchers' left, as the turn panel's dial draws it, and ↻ with a negative angle a turn to their right. On a narrow bar the counts, then the angle, are left out; point at the bar to read it all.

To face back on count 3, turning over the 2 counts before it:

<Steps>

1. Turn the marchers to the back on count 3, as above. They stay selected, so their row shows; its diamond has a small tab with an arrow on its left.
2. Drag the tab two counts to the left. A turn grows in front of the diamond, from count 1 to count 3, and the diamond stays on count 3. The turn goes at an even speed per count.

</Steps>

<Media
  id="marchers-facing-stretch"
  alt="G6 to G10 face side 2 at the end of Page 1, instantly. The small tab on the left of their diamond in the Facing lane is pulled two counts to the left, and a pale turn bar appears in front of the diamond, which stays where it was."
/>

- To change how many counts a turn takes, drag the turn, or its left edge. The facing change stays on its count. Dragged back to the diamond, the turn is instant again.
- It lands where you let go, and on page flags, the playhead and measures as other timeline edges do; hold <Kbd keys="Alt" gesture="drag" /> to turn that off.
- A turn can't start before those marchers' previous facing change: it stops there.

## Edit a turn

Select the marchers so their row shows, then double-click their turn's bar in the <Ui k="cues.lane.facing" plain /> lane, or right-click it and choose <Ui k="actions.cues.editTurn" />. A diamond in the top row with one facing change opens the same panel when you double-click it.

<Media
  id="marchers-facing-popover"
  alt="The turn panel for G6–G10's turn into the end of Page 1: a dial with Front at the bottom, Back at the top and S1 and S2 at the sides, its arc from the front to side 2, then From 0°, To 90° and Counts 2, and the chips Front, Back, Side 1 and Side 2 with Side 2 chosen."
/>

- Drag the dot on the dial to where they end up. Keep going past a full circle to add a spin.
- **From** is where they face when the turn starts. You can't change it here: it comes from their facing before.
- Type the angle they end at in **To**. It's on the facing change's count.
- <Ui k="cues.turn.counts" plain /> is how many counts the turn takes before that count (0 is instant). Changing it moves where the turn starts, not where it ends. This is also the way to set a turn's length without dragging.
- The chips turn them to a direction, the shorter way round for each marcher, as the menu does.

## Move, change or delete a facing change

- Drag a diamond in the top row sideways to move every facing change on that count; each keeps its own turn. To move one group's alone, select them and drag the diamond in their row, or the solid bar after it.
- Drag a group's diamond, or the solid bar after it, sideways to move that facing change to another count. Its turn moves with it, taking the same number of counts. To move it without dragging, put the playhead on the new count, right-click the bar and choose <Ui k="actions.cues.moveToPlayhead" />.
- Click a bar to pick its facing change. As when you click a move's bar, that also selects its counts: the start flag goes to where the turn starts and the playhead to those marchers' next facing change.
- Right-click a bar or a diamond, or press and hold it on a tablet, for its menu: <Ui k="actions.cues.editTurn" plain />, <Ui k="actions.cues.addSelected" plain />, <Ui k="actions.cues.removeSelected" plain />, <Ui k="cues.lane.selectPerformers" plain />, <Ui k="actions.cues.moveToPlayhead" plain /> and <Ui k="actions.cues.delete" plain />.
- A diamond in the top row that several groups share has a shorter menu instead: <Ui k="cues.lane.keySelectAll" plain />, then one entry per group to select only that group.
- Click a row's name to select its marchers, and only their row stays.

## Edit some of a group

Edits follow the selection. When you select only some of the marchers who share a facing change, their row's name says how many, such as "Trumpets 3 of 12", and editing it changes only the selected ones:

- Dragging the row's diamond or solid bar, or its turn, and <Ui k="actions.cues.moveToPlayhead" plain />, move or stretch only their facing change.
- The turn panel edits only theirs; its title says so, such as "T1–T3 (3 of 12)".

The selected marchers split off into a facing change of their own, and the others keep theirs, as one step of undo. So you can see the split, the others' row stays under yours, dimmed, until you change the selection: click its name to select them. A drag on a diamond in the top row still moves every facing change on that count.

To edit the whole group again, click the row's name ("Select all 12") first. With everyone in it selected, or nobody, an edit changes the whole facing change.

To join them back, make their facing change match the others' again on the same count, by dragging it back or setting the same turn: the two become one. Or select them, right-click the others' dimmed row (its bars don't drag, but have the menu) and choose <Ui k="actions.cues.addSelected" plain />: the marchers you add leave their own facing change that it replaces, on the same count or a few counts off (between the group's facing changes before and after), and one left with nobody is deleted.

## See facing on the field

To see which way everyone faces, select <Ui k="fieldDisplay.overlays" /> (the eye) in the bar at the field's top right and turn on <Ui k="fieldDisplay.facing" />. It's off when you start; turn it off again to hide the carets. The View menu has it too, and the command palette has <Ui k="actions.ui.showFacingArrows" /> and <Ui k="actions.ui.hideFacingArrows" />.

While it's on, every marcher has a small caret (^) in front of their dot, its point aimed the way they face: down the field toward the audience for the front, up for the back, and toward side 1 or side 2 for the sides. Marchers without a facing change face the front, so their carets point down; bass drums and flub drums start on side 2, so theirs point toward side 2.

<Media
  id="marchers-facing-field"
  alt="The field at the end of Page 1 with Facing on in Show on field. G6 to G10, along the front of the field, have carets on their right, pointing toward side 2. G14, G19 and G22 have carets above their dots, pointing up the field to the back, with their drill numbers moved below their dots. Every other marcher's caret is below their dot, pointing down to the front."
  maxWidth={720}
/>

The carets follow the playhead: as you scrub or play through a turn, they rotate at the same even speed as the turn, the way the turn goes, and land on the facing change's count. It's the same facing the <Ui k="cues.lane.facing" plain /> lane and the 3D View show.

- The carets are drawn in the field's label color with an edge in the field's background color, so they read over the grid in every field color scheme. Zoomed out to the whole field, they keep a size you can read.
- A drill number moves to the other side of its dot when the caret points through it: below the dot for a marcher facing the back.
- Props and equipment don't get a caret.

With <Ui k="settings.field.iconPlace.dot" plain /> chosen for <Ui k="settings.field.iconPlace" plain /> in <Ui k="settings.title" plain /> › <Ui k="settings.field" plain />, the caret sits outside the equipment icon instead of the dot ([Change what performers hold](/guides/marchers/equipment/)).

## In the 3D View

Marchers turn their chest and what they hold to the facing, and their feet follow the drill. When they face one way and travel another, they slide, march backward or step at an angle, whichever way they face. A slide across the field steps forward toward the 50 yard line and backward away from it. The battery keep their crab step. A turn over counts ends on the facing change's count, at an even speed; an instant turn snaps on its count.
