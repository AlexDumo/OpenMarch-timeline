On every page, each marcher is <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" />, <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" /> or <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" />. The state decides what happens to the marcher on this page when you change an earlier one. Most of the time you don't need to think about it: a marcher you move on a page is Defined there, and one you haven't moved yet is Not defined.

## The three states

- <Ui k="timeline.terms.defined" />: you've set the marcher's position on this page, with a <Term id="keyframe">keyframe</Term>. It reaches that spot at the page's flag, wherever it was before. Changing an earlier page changes how it gets here, not where it ends up.
- <Ui k="timeline.terms.notDefined" />: nothing has been decided for the marcher on this page. It stands where it last was, and when you change that earlier position, it moves here too. Every marcher on a new page is Not defined, and so is every marcher you add: they stand where the last set left them.
- <Ui k="timeline.terms.held" />: you've chosen to keep the marcher still, with a hold. A hold grows out of whatever last put the marcher there: the keyframe where it last arrived, or its starting spot if it hasn't moved since the start of the show. Like a Not defined marcher, it follows changes to that keyframe. The difference is what a drag does: see [Drag marchers that are Held](#drag-marchers-that-are-held).

Use Held when the marcher's standing still is part of the drill and should stay that way. Use Not defined, the default, for marchers you haven't got to yet. [Make marchers stand still](/guides/timeline/holds/) shows how to hold them.

## How you can tell

Select marchers, and the timeline marks their states along the bottom edge of the page row, as in the picture above:

- A diamond marks each count where they have a keyframe. It's filled when every selected marcher has one there, and hollow when only some do.
- A thin line runs from a keyframe over the counts where they stand still: solid where they're Held, dashed where they're Not defined. Where some are Held and some Not defined over the same counts, it's dashed. A shape carried onto later pages draws a solid line too.
- A short upright tick marks where they move again.

On the field, a marcher is drawn with a dashed outline where it's Not defined at the playhead, and solid otherwise. A marcher's label is bold at its keyframes and lighter between them.

The inspector says it in words, for example <Ui k="inspector.marcher.timeline.heldSince" values={{ held: "Held", page: 3 }} plain />, with a link to that page. When only some of the selection is Held, the editing line over the field says how many, for example "Editing Page 4's move <Ui k="timeline.windowLine.held" values={{ count: 1, total: 2, held: "Held" }} plain />", and a drag edits each marcher by its own state.

## Later pages keep where they were

When you change a page, later pages where the marchers are Defined keep them where they were. Later pages where they're Not defined or Held show the change, since those pages follow the last keyframe. When a drag changes later pages this way, a message names them, with a button to keep them as before.

While you drag marchers on the field, the lines of the later pages that will change grow thicker and turn the accent color, so you can see what moves before you let go.

Commands that take a position away (<Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" />, <Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" />) leave later pages where they were too. Where a later page showed the position you're taking away, the menu offers a second item ending in "move back too", which lets those pages go back with it; pointing at it lights up the lines of those pages.

## Drag marchers that are Held

A drag on a page inside a hold moves the whole hold, including the keyframe it grows out of: the marchers stand at the new spot from that keyframe, or from the start of the show, to the end of the hold. While you drag, the whole line lights up. For example, in Basic, trumpets Held on Pages 4 and 5 and dragged on Page 5 move on Page 4 too; if they were Not defined on Page 5, the drag would give them a move into Page 5 instead.

In Expert, the start flag decides it. A drag whose start flag is at or before the hold's start moves the whole hold, as above; selecting the hold's first page puts the start flag there. A drag whose start flag is later than the hold's start, such as on a later page of a hold over several pages, ends the hold at the start flag and adds a move from there to the playhead. While you drag, the line stays dark before the start flag and lights from it on, so you can see which will happen.

## Define marchers where they stand

<Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" /> makes Not defined marchers Defined where they stand now, so later changes to earlier pages no longer move them on this page. Held marchers stay Held.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the page's box, or the dashed line on it, and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.defineAsShown" />.

Dragging a Not defined marcher also makes it Defined, at the spot you drag it to.

## Clear a page's positions

<Ui k="timeline.terms.clearThisPage" /> takes the marchers' positions off the page, so they're Not defined there again and stand where the page before left them. It also takes away a hold on the page, so it's how you remove one.

1. Select the marchers. To clear the page for everyone, select nobody.
2. Right-click the page's box and select <Ui k="actions.batchEdit.clearPage" values={{ page: 3 }} /> (with the page's number, and "(all marchers)" after it when nobody is selected), or press <Kbd action="setSelectedMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for the selected marchers or <Kbd action="setAllMarchersToPreviousPage" /> for everyone.

The same command is in the inspector, as <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeSelectedMarchers" /> › <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.toPreviousPagePositions" />, and for every marcher in the toolbar's <Ui k="inspector.shape.alignmentTab" /> tab, under <Ui k="toolbar.alignment.placeAllMarchers" />.

A hold that grew out of the cleared page's keyframe goes too, and a message says so.

## Remove one keyframe

<Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" /> takes away one position at one count, where Clear this page works on a whole page. Use it on a keyframe in the middle of a page.

1. Select the marchers.
2. Right-click the diamond at the count and select <Ui k="timeline.terms.removeKeyframe" />.

The marchers no longer stop at that spot: they walk from the keyframe before it to the keyframe after it. Removing the keyframe a hold grows out of removes the hold too. In Basic, a position in the middle of a page is a tick instead; see [Set a position in the middle of a page](/guides/timeline/moves/#set-a-position-in-the-middle-of-a-page-basic).
